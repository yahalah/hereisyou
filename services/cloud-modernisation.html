Cloud modernisation detail

We start by classifying applications by criticality, data sensitivity, and integration complexity. This allows us to prioritise migration waves in a way that aligns to business impact and regulatory expectations.

Architecture decisions are documented in decision logs, including trade-offs between rehosting, replatforming, and refactoring. This transparency helps steering committees manage risk and cost.

We implement service catalogues and standardised landing zone templates so new workloads inherit monitoring, logging, and security defaults without delay.

Our migration factory includes testing automation, data validation, and cutover rehearsal to reduce the risk of production incidents.

FinOps governance is embedded with tagging, budget alerts, and monthly optimisation reviews to prevent cost drift after go-live.

Operational handover includes runbooks, escalation paths, and ownership mapping so internal teams can support the new estate confidently.

We conduct resilience testing to verify RTO and RPO targets, ensuring the new platform meets operational resilience obligations.

Post-migration, we run optimisation sprints to reduce technical debt, improve performance, and align observability with service-level objectives.
